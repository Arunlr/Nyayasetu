# Nyayasetu

## Tooth Care Speciality & Family Dental Clinic — Website

A single-page website recreating the design & animation language of the supplied
`3d dentist design.mp4` reference (cream / brown / lavender / lime palette,
masked-line headline reveals, rotating circular badge, services marquee +
auto-expanding accordion, scoop section transition, infinite specialists
carousel, staggered "Who We Help" cards, reviews carousel, and a lime
"Book appointment today" footer revealed with a circle-wipe), rebranded for
Tooth Care Speciality & Family Dental Clinic, Horamavu–Kalkere, Bengaluru,
with its live Google Maps location integrated (embedded map, Directions and
Google listing links — 13.0560111, 77.6525715).

Run: `python3 -m http.server 8000` then open http://localhost:8000

Note: patient names/quotes in the Reviews section are illustrative placeholders
(themed on common patient feedback) — replace them with the clinic's real
Google reviews, and add the clinic's phone number / confirmed hours when available.
