Comprehensive Clinical Scope

Eight Dedicated  Clinical Departments.

From everyday tooth-colored fillings and painless root canals to specialist oral lesion screening, laser gum therapy, clear aligners, implants, and childhood caries care.

Dept 01

Management of Tooth Decay

Conservative, natural-looking restorations and comfortable endodontic care designed to save your natural tooth.

  • Tooth colored restorations
  • Root canal treatment (RCT)
Dept 02

Management of Tooth Loss

Fixed and removable prosthodontic replacements engineered for natural bite strength, speech, and aesthetics.

  • Crown and Bridge restorations
  • Removable dentures
  • Tooth implants
Dept 03 Specialist Oral Medicine

Management of Oral Disease

Rare specialist diagnosis and non-surgical management of mucosal conditions, jaw joint pain, and early screening.

  • Diagnosis & treatment of premalignant lesions of the oral cavity
  • Screening for Oral malignant lesions
  • Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) diseases
  • Other soft tissue lesions of the oral cavity
  • Management of Orofacial pain
Dept 04

Management of Periodontal Disease

Gentle ultrasonic cleaning and modern laser-assisted gum care to stop bleeding gums and protect bone support.

  • Periodontal therapy (Laser and non-Laser)
  • Oral prophylaxis (Tooth cleaning)
Dept 05

Management of Malposed Teeth

Structured tooth alignment for teens and adults using precision braces or discreet removable clear aligners.

  • Orthodontic therapy (Braces)
  • Clear aligners
Dept 06

Management of Dental Esthetics

Enamel-safe brightening and conservative aesthetic enhancements tailored harmoniously to your facial features.

  • Teeth whitening
  • Cosmetic dentistry
Dept 07

Surgical Management

Careful, atraumatic extractions and minor oral surgery performed under strict aseptic protocols and gentle anesthesia.

  • Teeth extraction
  • Wisdom tooth extraction
  • Minor surgical procedures
Dept 08

Management of Childhood Caries

Patient, child-friendly preventive sealants and pediatric pulp therapy that keep growing smiles healthy and fear-free.

  • Pit and fissure sealants
  • Pulpectomy and pulpotomy procedures
