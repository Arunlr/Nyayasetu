Our Dental Services

Comprehensive Treatments  Tailored to Every Smile.

Every treatment department at Shridha Dental Clinic combines thorough diagnosis, modern laser and restorative technology, and unhurried chairside comfort for children, adults, and seniors.

01 · Tooth Decay Care
Management of Tooth Decay

Root Canal & Tooth-Colored Fillings

Painless endodontic root canal therapy and natural shade-matched composite restorations designed to save your natural tooth.

  • Painless Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
  • Natural tooth-colored composite restorations
02 · Tooth Replacement
Management of Tooth Loss

Dental Implants, Crowns & Bridges

Precision ceramic caps, fixed bridges, titanium tooth implants, and comfortable dentures engineered for natural bite strength.

  • Crown & Bridge restorations and cap fixation
  • Titanium tooth implants
  • Comfortable removable dentures
03 · Specialist Dept Dr. Veena · Oral Medicine
Management of Oral Disease

Oral Medicine, TMJ & Pain Diagnosis

Specialist evaluation of oral mucosal conditions, jaw joint pain, and early preventive screening led by an Oral Medicine professor.

  • Diagnosis & care of premalignant oral lesions
  • Screening for oral malignant lesions
  • Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders
  • Soft tissue lesions & Orofacial pain management
04 · Gum & Laser Care
Management of Periodontal Disease

Laser Gum Care & Teeth Cleaning

Gentle ultrasonic scaling, stain removal, and modern laser-assisted periodontal therapy for healthy, non-bleeding gums.

  • Periodontal gum therapy (Laser & non-Laser)
  • Ultrasonic oral prophylaxis (Tooth cleaning & polishing)
05 · Orthodontics
Management of Malposed Teeth

Braces & Clear Aligners

Guided tooth straightening and bite alignment for teens and working adults using precision braces or discreet clear aligners.

  • Discreet removable Clear Aligners
  • Orthodontic therapy (Metal & ceramic braces)
06 · Smile Esthetics
Management of Dental Esthetics

Teeth Whitening & Cosmetic Dentistry

Enamel-safe professional brightening and conservative smile aesthetics crafted naturally around your facial harmony.

  • Professional enamel-safe Teeth Whitening
  • Aesthetic bonding & Cosmetic dentistry
07 · Oral Surgery
Surgical Management

Wisdom Tooth & Minor Oral Surgery

Safe, atraumatic extractions and impacted wisdom tooth procedures under strict sterile protocols and gentle local anesthesia.

  • Impacted wisdom tooth extraction
  • Atraumatic painless tooth extractions
  • Minor oral surgical procedures
08 · Pediatric Care
Management of Childhood Caries

Kids & Preventive Dentistry

Warm, fear-free dental visits for children focused on early cavity protection, gentle pulp care, and lifelong healthy habits.

  • Preventive pit & fissure sealants
  • Pediatric pulpectomy & pulpotomy procedures
